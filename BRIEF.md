# Project Brief
A modern content strategy dashboard for Banana Books. Banana Books is a small, local bookstore that specializes in young adult fiction and graphic novels. They want to garner a larger social media following on TikTok so that they can grow as a business. This dashboard will be used primarily on a tablet device by a social media manager. 

## Tech
- Create a new Vue project
- Deploys to Vercel as a static site 

## Design
- Header with name of company
- Subheader that explains the purpose of dashboard
- Layout grid of cards with metrics
- Clear visual hierarchy and ample white space
- Date filter that adjusts data displayed
- Use yellow and orange gradients
- Google Font: Lato

## Metrics to Display
- Content views
- Content engagement rates
- Ad revenue
- Subscriber churn
- Audience insights
- Best times to post

## Nice to Haves
- Trending audio and video ideas
- Save ideas for future
- Animations for graphs and charts
